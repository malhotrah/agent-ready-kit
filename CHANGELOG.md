# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-02)
- Initial release: the `/agent-ready` skill (scan, interview, plan, generate, merge, validate) with `audit` and subtree modes.
- Templates for AGENTS.md (root and nested), CLAUDE.md, engineering docs, ADRs, `.claude` settings, hooks, subagents and commands, plus Copilot and Cursor pointers.
- `install.ps1` (Windows) and `install.sh` (macOS/Linux/Git Bash) installers with force and uninstall options.
